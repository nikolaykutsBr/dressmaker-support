Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026

In short: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your game stays on your iPhone. We use anonymous analytics to understand how the game is played, and Apple handles all payments.

This policy explains what information the iPhone app Dressmaker: Sewing & Fashion (the “App”), published by Mikalai Kuts (“we”, “us”), collects and why.

What we collect

InformationWhere it goesWhy
Game progress: dresses you made, coins, ordersStays on your device onlyTo keep your game between launches
Usage events: screens opened, orders taken and delivered, subscription offer shown, purchase outcomeGoogle Firebase AnalyticsTo understand which parts of the game work and improve them
App instance identifier, app version, device model, iOS version, language, approximate countryGoogle Firebase AnalyticsTo count installs and sessions without knowing who you are
Purchase status (whether a subscription is active)Read on your device from Apple’s StoreKitTo unlock subscription content

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any content you create. The App has no account or sign-in and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, do not show ads, and do not sell or share personal data.

Analytics

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics (the version without advertising identifier support). The data is not linked to your identity and is processed by Google LLC as our service provider under Google’s Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy terms. Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months.

Payments

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to payments.

Permissions

The App does not ask for any system permission. “Save image” opens the standard iOS share sheet; the App never reads your photo library.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold information that identifies you, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. Deleting the App removes your game progress and resets the analytics identifier. You can contact us with any privacy request, including requests under the GDPR or the CCPA, and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
