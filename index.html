Dressmaker: Sewing & Fashion is a cozy dress-atelier game for iPhone. Clients bring their orders, you design the dress in your sketchbook — bodice, sleeves, skirt, fabrics and trims — and add it to your gallery.

Frequently asked

How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone?
Open any screen with the subscription offer and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge.

How do I get a refund?
Payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my progress saved?
Your dresses, coins and orders are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Something looks wrong?
Tell us what happened, your iPhone model and iOS version — a screenshot helps a lot.
